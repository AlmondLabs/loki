## Priority issues
1. [P1] A turn splits in two at the New line or a day change (entries.ts:147 closes the turn on any day/new marker). Fix: markers become parts inside a turn; only a user message ends one.
2. [P1] Finished steps drawn as "pending" at muted/50 (~2.1:1 day, ~2.85:1 night; work.tsx:155, chain-of-thought.tsx:81). Breaks AA and design.md's no-opacity-dim rule. Fix: housekeeping steps "complete" or a quiet status at full alpha.
3. [P1] The approval is a red-tinted panel (proposal.tsx:37) against design.md "red only a badge or dot, never a panel"; in Flow its green approve competes with green archive. Placeholder says "below" for buttons above (conversation.tsx:513). Fix: card surface + red dot/badge; quiet ActionBar main while a proposal is open; fix the placeholder.
4. [P2] An approve/deny leaves no trace: the step vanishes. Fix: keep the step, closed line reads "approved by you 12:52" / "denied" (KTD6).
5. [P2] Status repeated and failure over-reported: "failed" from any failed step (entries.ts:143). Fix: badge only when the last step failed or the turn errored; drop the "thinking" trace under a working badge; fold an unchanged plan to one line.
